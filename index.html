English

Math Heroes is a math game for children made by ALILOU DIGITAL. We respect your child's privacy.

  • We collect no personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • The app saves only its own settings on the device (chosen language, sound on/off, and which challenges were won). This information never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is removed.
  • The app does not use the internet, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
